## What this assistant does not cover

This assistant is built for **resident individual** taxpayers with **salary**, **house
property** (net rental income you supply), and **bank / financial-asset interest** you
enter as a single amount. It does **not** compute business or professional income, capital
gains, foreign income, agricultural income, non-resident rules, company or firm taxation,
or net-wealth surcharge amounts. It also does **not** file a return on your behalf — it
only explains rules and runs arithmetic from your inputs.

## When to use a tax professional

If your return needs firm or AoP income, offshore property, voluntary disclosure under
the 7th schedule, or complex asset schedules beyond what you can express as simple
inputs, use a qualified practitioner. The Ask tab may still describe **portal process**
(e.g. how to register) but will not invent figures for heads we do not model.
