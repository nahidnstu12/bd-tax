## Why does my source tax show as unverified?

Only **three** claim types are auto-verified by NBR systems: **Car AIT**, **iBAS++
salary** source tax, and **Sanchayapatra**. Other claims — including **Salary Others**
for many private employers — may stay unverified until a tax officer reviews them. Online
verification depends on the withholding agent’s system being connected; it can take from
seconds to minutes.

## Where do I claim TDS on the portal?

Use **Update Tax Payment Status** to open the **e-Return Ledger**. Claim menus include
Salary iBAS++, Salary Others, bank or FI interest, Sanchayapatra, and others. **Sync
From Income** pulls figures where the income tabs already captured them. Return to e-Return
via **Tax Payment Status → Go to eReturn** when finished.
