## How can I pay tax due on e-Return?

If Tax & Payment shows an amount payable, use **Pay Now**. Methods include **Internet
Banking**, **Card**, and **Mobile Banking** (Rocket, bKash, Nagad, Upay, tap, CellFin).
Gateways include **e-Chalan** and **Sonali Bank Payment**. After payment the portal
redirects back to e-Return automatically.

## What is the difference between payable, payment, and refundable?

The final block shows **Total Amount Payable**, **Total Payment** (source tax, advance
tax, and payment with return), **Refundable**, and **Payable** after netting. Our
calculator models source tax and advance tax against net tax only; surcharge, penalty,
and prior-year refund adjustment on the official form are out of scope for v1.
