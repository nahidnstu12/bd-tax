## What are the steps to file a regular e-Return online?

From the portal home go to **Submission → Regular e-Return**. Complete seven tabs in
order, using **Save & Continue** between tabs and **Save Draft** anytime:

Assessment → Income → Rebate → Expenditure → Assets & Liabilities → Tax & Payment →
Return View.

## Which heads of income can I select on the Assessment tab?

The portal lists Employment, Rent, Agriculture, Business, Capital Gains, Income from
Financial Assets, and Other Sources. Enable only what applies. Additional checkboxes cover
partner income, AoP, foreign income, and spouse or minor children’s income. Our calculator
v1 focuses on employment, house property, and financial-asset interest entered as amounts.

## When is the IT-10B assets statement required?

On Additional Information the portal asks IT-10B triggers: gross wealth over 50 lakh,
motor car, offshore property, shareholder director status, or house property in a city
corporation. Answering yes may require a full assets and liabilities schedule. Wealth
reconciliation must show **difference zero** before submit.

## How do I submit after payment?

On Tax & Payment, claim source tax and pay if due, then **Proceed to Online Return**.
Preview the official **IT-11GA** form in English or Bangla, then **Submit Return**.
Download acknowledgement and certificates from the Tax Record menu afterward.
