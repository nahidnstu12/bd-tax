## What can I download after submitting a return?

From the **Tax Record** menu you can download **TIN Certificate**, **Express Certificate
/ PSR Verification Request**, **Certificate Status**, **Acknowledgement**, the filed
**Return**, **Challan**, and **History**. The certificate often appears immediately after
successful submission.

## What form does the online preview use?

The preview renders **IT-11GA (2023)** — *Form of Return of Income for Individual
Person* — in English or Bangla. Header fields include name, NID or passport, TIN, circle,
taxes zone, assessment year, and residential status. Use **Back** from preview to fix data
before final submit.
