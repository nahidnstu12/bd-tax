## What do I need before I can register on e-Return?

You need a **TIN** and a **biometric-verified mobile SIM** registered against your NID.
Both are mandatory. The portal sends a 6-digit OTP to that mobile during registration and
when resetting a password.

## How do I register for an e-Return account?

On the sign-in page choose **Register**, enter TIN, biometric-verified mobile number, and
captcha, then **Verify**. After OTP confirmation, set a password and submit. You are then
redirected to sign in with TIN and password.

## I changed my mobile number — what now?

Use **Change mobile number** on the sign-in page. Enter TIN and the **new** number; it
must also be biometric-verified. Complete OTP and password confirmation. If OTP fails,
the number is not linked to your NID records the way NBR expects.
