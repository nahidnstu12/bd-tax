## Which investments qualify for rebate on e-Return?

Twelve categories appear on the Rebate tab: life insurance, DPS, approved Sanchayapatra,
unit certificates and mutual funds, listed shares, GPF, RPF, approved superannuation,
benevolent fund and group insurance, Zakat Fund, universal pension, and others. Declare
amounts made during the income year; the portal shows total actual and **allowable**
investment for rebate.

## How is the investment rebate calculated for 2025-26?

The rebate is the **lowest of three** amounts: **3% of total income** (rebate base wording
still marked open in config as V1), **15% of eligible investment**, and an **absolute cap
of 10,00,000 taka**. Only the winning leg applies. If you have no eligible investment,
rebate is zero. The calculator reports which leg bound (`income`, `investment`, or `cap`).

## Does bank interest affect the rebate percentage leg?

Total income for the 3% leg includes taxable salary after the one-third exemption plus
other heads you enter (house property, bank interest, other income). Investment can still
cap the rebate even when the 3% leg would be larger — check the bound leg in the
calculator breakdown.
