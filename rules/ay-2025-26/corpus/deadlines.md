## What is the income year for Assessment Year 2025-26?

For AY **2025-26**, the **income year** runs **1 July 2024 through 30 June 2025** (twelve
months). This matches the Assessment screen in the NBR e-Return User Manual and
`rules/ay-2025-26/config.json`. Salary, rent, and interest should align to that period
unless you use partial employment dates on the portal for a mid-year job start.

## When can I file the 2025-26 return online?

Online **Regular e-Return** is available from the portal when NBR opens the assessment
year; older years may remain selectable for late filing. This repo does **not** store a
statutory **last filing date** for 2025-26 in config — check NBR notices and the portal
for the current deadline and any penalty rules.

## How do income year and assessment year differ?

**Assessment year** (e.g. 2025-26) is the tax year label on the return. **Income year** is
the 12-month window whose income you report (usually the July–June period ending just before
the assessment cycle). Always pick the assessment year that matches the income year you are
declaring.
