## What is the income year for Assessment Year 2026-27?

Income year **1 July 2025 through 30 June 2026** for AY **2026-27**, per
`rules/ay-2026-27/config.json`. Select **2026-27** on the portal Assessment tab and confirm
the income-year dates before entering employment and other heads.

## What is the filing deadline for 2026-27?

Config records **`filing_deadline`: 2026-11-30** (commentary / research capture — confirm on
NBR circular before relying on it). Late filing may affect **investment rebate**: policy
video E2b claims rebate is only if you file in the **first quarter (Jul–Sep)** of the filing
season, with no rebate Oct–Dec and penalties later — stored as `_filing_rebate_quarters`,
**not applied in the calculator**.

## Filing season vs income year

You report income earned in the July–June **income year**, but you **file** in the following
months when the assessment year opens (typically after the budget cycle). Keep assessment
year, income year, and any quarter-based rebate rules straight when planning investments and
submission timing.
