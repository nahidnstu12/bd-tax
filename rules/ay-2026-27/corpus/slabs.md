## How is income taxed above the exempt threshold in 2026-27?

Tax applies only to total income **above** your category threshold. Band **widths** are the
same shape as 2025-26; the main change for many filers is the **higher general threshold**
(4,00,000 vs 3,75,000). The calculator applies bands to the excess, rounds to whole taka,
then applies rebate and minimum tax.

## What are the slab bands and rates for 2026-27?

Above the exempt threshold, in order:

1. **Next 3,00,000 taka** at **10%**
2. **Next 4,00,000 taka** at **15%**
3. **Next 5,00,000 taka** at **20%**
4. **Next 20,00,000 taka** at **25%**
5. **Remainder** at **30%**

Policy video examples (e.g. taxable 5,00,000 → 10,000 tax; taxable 8,00,000 → 45,000)
align with this structure plus the 4,00,000 zero band. Slab config is still **unverified**
against a filed return with non-zero gross tax.

## What changed from 2025-26 for slab tax?

Commentary and E2b describe a **higher tax-free slice** (4,00,000 general threshold) and
**weaker investment rebate** (10% and 7,50,000 cap vs 15% and 10,00,000). Band widths and
top rates in config match 2025-26; a taxpayer can owe **more** in 2026-27 despite a higher
threshold if rebate was investment-bound in the prior year.
