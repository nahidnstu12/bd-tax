## What is the tax-free threshold for a general taxpayer in 2026-27?

The general exempt threshold rises to **4,00,000 taka** of total income (from 3,75,000 in
2025-26 per commentary and policy video). Slab tax applies only on income above that
amount plus any category-specific threshold.

## What changed in the slab structure?

After the threshold, bands are **3,00,000 at 10%**, **4,00,000 at 15%**, **5,00,000 at
20%**, **20,00,000 at 25%**, and **30% on the remainder** — same widths as 2025-26 with a
higher entry threshold. Slab arithmetic in the calculator is **unverified** against a
filed return with tax payable.

## Category thresholds for 2026-27

Female or senior 65+ **4,25,000**; person with disability, third gender, and gazetted
freedom fighter **5,25,000** in config (partially assumed from 2025-26 structure — see V3
in config `_verify`). Parent-of-disabled extra **50,000** still configured but unconfirmed
(V5).
