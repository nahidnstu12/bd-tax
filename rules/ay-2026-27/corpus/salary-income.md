## How is salary exemption calculated for Assessment Year 2026-27?

The calculator uses the same shape as 2025-26: **one-third of gross salary** exempt
(whole taka), with **absolute cap 4,50,000 taka** (unverified). Taxable salary is gross
minus exempt amount. No separate Finance Act citation is in the repo for 2026-27 yet; this
follows the filed 2025-26 return and portal employment demos.

## What should I enter from my salary certificate?

Enter annual totals for basic, allowances, bonus, and non-cash benefits. Multiple employers
use separate employment rows on the portal; for the calculator you may merge into one gross
salary total for the year unless you are testing cap edge cases.
