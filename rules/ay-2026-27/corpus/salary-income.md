## How is salary exemption calculated for Assessment Year 2026-27?

The calculator uses the same shape as 2025-26: **one-third of gross salary** exempt
(whole taka), with **absolute cap 4,50,000 taka** (unverified). Taxable salary is gross
minus exempt amount. No separate Finance Act citation is in the repo for 2026-27 yet; this
follows the filed 2025-26 return and portal employment demos.

## What counts as salary income for 2026-27?

Salary income covers basic pay, allowances, **festival bonus**, and non-cash benefits, all
entered as annual totals. These add up to gross salary, which is the figure the one-third
exemption above is applied to. Nothing about what counts as salary changed from 2025-26.

## What if I had more than one employer this year?

Multiple employers use separate employment rows on the portal, one per employer. For the
calculator you may merge them into a single gross salary total for the year, unless you are
specifically testing behaviour at the absolute cap, where per-employer treatment could
differ.

## What should I enter from my salary certificate?

Enter **annual totals** from the certificate — basic, allowances, bonus, and non-cash
benefits — rather than monthly figures. The salary certificate covers the full income year,
and its yearly totals are what the portal expects under the employment head.
