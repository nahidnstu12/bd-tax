## 3. Figures by year

### 3.1 Tax-free thresholds

| Category | **AY 2025-26** (S1) | **AY 2026-27** (S2 / S3) |
|---|---|---|
| General | **375,000** | **400,000** |
| Women / senior citizen (65+) | 425,000 | 425,000 (S3) |
| Persons with disabilities | 500,000 | *(S3 groups — see below)* |
| Third gender | 500,000 | *(S3 groups)* |
| Gazetted freedom fighter | 525,000 | *(S3 groups)* |
| Disabled / third gender / freedom fighter (grouped) | — | "up to **525,000**" (S3) |
| Parent/guardian of disabled child | **+50,000** additional | *(not stated)* |

S2 states only that special categories "may qualify for higher thresholds under
applicable law" — no figures. S3's grouped "up to 525,000" is less precise than S1's
per-category breakdown; **the per-category split for AY 2026-27 must come from the Act.**

### 3.2 Slab bands

**AY 2025-26 (S1):**

| Band | Rate |
|---|---|
| First 375,000 | 0% |
| Next 300,000 | 10% |
| Next 400,000 | 15% |
| Next 500,000 | 20% |
| Next 2,000,000 | 25% |
| Remaining | 30% |

**AY 2026-27 (S2 and S3, identical to each other):**

| Band | Rate |
|---|---|
| First 400,000 | 0% |
| Next 300,000 | 10% |
| Next 400,000 | 15% |
| Next 500,000 | 20% |
| Next 2,000,000 | 25% |
| Remaining | 30% |

Rates and all band widths after the first are unchanged year over year. **Only the
tax-free threshold moved: 375,000 → 400,000.**

### 3.3 Investment rebate

| Component | **AY 2025-26** (S1) | **AY 2026-27** (S2 / S3) |
|---|---|---|
| Rate on eligible investment | **15%** | **10%** |
| % of income cap | 3% of total income | 3% of taxable income |
| Absolute ceiling | **1,000,000** | **750,000** |
| Rule | lowest of the three applies | lowest of the three applies |

Note the wording difference — S1 says *total* income, S2/S3 say *taxable* income. These
are not the same base. **Verify which applies in each year.**

Eligible investments (S1, fullest list): DPS, life insurance premium, provident fund,
Sanchaypatra, listed stocks / mutual funds, Sukuk, government securities.
S2/S3 mention life insurance premiums, savings certificates, DPS.

### 3.4 Minimum tax (individuals)

| Case | AY 2025-26 (S1) | AY 2026-27 (S3) |
|---|---|---|
| Standard taxpayer | 5,000 flat | 5,000 flat |
| First-time / new filer | 1,000 | 1,000 |

Appears unchanged across both years.

S1 notes location tiers (Dhaka / other city corporation / rest) were **replaced by a flat
nationwide amount** — meaning the flat structure was already in force in AY 2025-26.

**Calculator policy:** flat **5,000** for both configured years; **E2a** zone table (3k/4k/5k)
is not modeled — see [`verification-findings.md`](../verification-findings.md) F2.

Trigger wording differs and must be resolved:
- S1: acts as a **floor when slab tax falls below** the amount
- S3: applies to individuals **exceeding the tax-free limit**

These are not equivalent rules for someone just above the threshold.

## 4. Year-over-year changes — AY 2025-26 → AY 2026-27

This is the diff the "what changed this year" feature should surface.

| Field | 2025-26 | 2026-27 | Direction |
|---|---|---|---|
| General tax-free threshold | 375,000 | 400,000 | ⬆️ **+25,000** — relief |
| First slab band width | 375,000 | 400,000 | ⬆️ follows threshold |
| Rebate rate on eligible investment | 15% | 10% | ⬇️ **−5pp** — less relief |
| Rebate absolute ceiling | 1,000,000 | 750,000 | ⬇️ **−250,000** |
| Slab rates (10/15/20/25/30%) | — | — | unchanged |
| Band widths above the first | — | — | unchanged |
| Minimum tax (5,000 / 1,000) | — | — | unchanged |

**Video-only (UNVERIFIED):** quarterly filing windows — investment rebate only if submitted
Jul–Sep; Oct–Dec filing loses rebate; later quarters add penalties. See
[`research/video-guide-ay-2026-27-policy-updates.md`](video-guide-ay-2026-27-policy-updates.md) §4 and drift index
[`research/video-guide.md`](video-guide.md). Not in S1–S3.

**Net effect:** a higher entry threshold, but materially weaker investment rebate. For a
taxpayer with significant eligible investment, 2026-27 may cost *more* despite the higher
threshold — a genuinely useful thing for the assistant to explain, and a good first test
of the calculator on both years.

any older year that might be added later — precisely the case year-folder versioning
